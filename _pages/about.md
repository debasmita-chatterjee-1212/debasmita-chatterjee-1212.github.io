I'm a Mechanical Engineering graduate from IIT Kharagpur (Class of 2023). I am a robotics researcher mainly focused on motion planning and autonomous vehicles, though I have dabbled in surgical robotics and multi-robot coordination. My work has taken me across four countries and five research groups — from simulating adaptive motion planning in dynamic terrains at Sorbonne Université, to building vision-based pursuit control systems for UAVs at the University of Alberta, to leading warehouse robotics design and designing surgical simulation platform for haptic devices in IIT Kharagpur.

 I'm currently interested in multi-robot interaction and coordination in challenging environments. I always look for ways to create and build my own robots from scratch. Feel free to reach out and discuss about robotics, autonomous vehicles or intelligent systems.
